# Glossary

**Terms, Expressions and Acronyms (TEA) must be organized alphabetically.**

Column "TEA (PT)" is optional. If the team does not intend to use it, then remove it.

(Complete according to the provided example)

| **_TEA_** (EN)                | **_TEA_** (PT)                          | **_Description_** (EN)                                                                                                                            |                                       
|:------------------------------|:----------------------------------------|:--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Base Value**                | **Valor Base**                          | A reference price used to calculate earnings from cargo deliveries, adjusted according to station demand.                                         |
| **Block Assignment**          | **Distribuição de Blocos**              | The process of placing house blocks around a city either manually or using a random distribution.                                                 |
| **Building**                  | **Edifício**                            | A structure that can be added to a station, to upgrade its statistics.                                                                            |
| **Cargo**                     | **Carga**                               | Materials that are transported by trains, between stations.                                                                                       |
| **Cargo Mode**                | **Modo de Carga**                       | A loading behavior for trains at a station, such as FULL, HALF, or AVAILABLE, that determines departure conditions.                               |
| **Cargo Type**                | **Tipo de Carga**                       | A category of goods or materials transported by train, such as coal, passengers, or steel.                                                        |
| **Cargo Value Calculation**   | **Cálculo de Valor da Carga**           | The process of determining delivery value based on base value, demand, distance, and station buildings.                                           |
| **Carriage**                  | **Carruagem**                           | A part of a train that is used to carry either people or freight.                                                                                 |
| **City**                      | **Cidade**                              | A location in the map that is able to generate various types of cargo, such as passengers and mail, and that can also consume the final products. |
| **City Name Rules**           | **Regras de Nome da Cidade**            | Restrictions applied to naming cities, excluding digits and special characters.                                                                   |
| **Connectivity**              | **Conectividade**                       | The ability of trains to travel between two stations within the railway network.                                                                  |
| **Cost**                      | **Custo**                               | The amount of budget that a certain action requires in order to be done.                                                                          |
| **Demand**                    | **Procura**                             | A dynamic metric representing the need for a specific cargo type at a station, affecting delivery profitability.                                  |
| **Demand Variation Rule**     | **Regra de Variação da Procura**        | The rule by which a station’s demand increases or decreases based on annual delivery volumes.                                                     |
| **Depot**                     | **Depósito**                            | A small station that costs 50k and has a 3x3 economic radius.                                                                                     |
| **Diesel Locomotive**         | **Locomotiva Diesel**                   | A type of locomotive in which the prime mover is a diesel engine.                                                                                 |
| **Double Line**               | **Linha Dupla**                         | A type of railway that allows trains to travel in both directions.                                                                                |
| **Editor**                    | **Editor**                              | A person that can create maps and scenarios.                                                                                                      |
| **Electric Locomotive**       | **Locomotiva Elétrica**                 | A type of locomotive that is powered by electricity, which requires specific electrified lines.                                                   |
| **Electrified Line**          | **Linha Eletrificada**                  | A railway line that allows operation of electric locomotives, usually more costly to build and maintain.                                          |
| **Farm**                      | **Quinta**                              | A facility that produces crops.                                                                                                                   |
| **Fuel Cost**                 | **Custo de Combustível**                | The cost based on distance traveled by a train, depending on the locomotive type.                                                                 |
| **Game Year**                 | **Ano de Jogo**                         | The in-game calendar year, used to trigger events like locomotive availability or building unlocks.                                               |
| **Geometric Center**          | **Centro Geométrico**                   | The central reference point used when placing a station on the map.                                                                               |
| **Goods**                     | **Bens**                                | General term for final products such as cars, steel, or textiles produced by transforming industries.                                             |
| **Graphical Visualization**   | **Visualização Gráfica**                | A visual representation of railway components such as stations and lines, often used for analysis.                                                |
| **Historical Restriction**    | **Restrição Histórica**                 | Scenario-defined event that alters gameplay, such as wars or campaigns affecting cargo demand.                                                    |
| **Historical Event**          | **Evento Histórico**                    | An event defined in a scenario that alters gameplay (e.g., war, vaccine campaign).                                                                |
| **House Blocks**              | **Blocos de Casas**                     | A type of structure that is part of a city and can be on the map, responsible to generate people and mail.                                        |
| **Import/Export Cargo**       | **Carga de Importação/Exportação**      | Cargo types that are brought into or sent out from a scenario via a port.                                                                         |
| **Industry**                  | **Indústria**                           | A structure that is located on the map that can consume or transform resources. Industries can be primary, transforming or mixed.                 |
| **Line**                      | **Linha**                               | A track connecting two stations.                                                                                                                  |
| **Location**                  | **Localização**                         | A pair of coordinates that are used to represent the position of a structure in the map.                                                          |
| **Locomotive**                | **Locomotiva**                          | The engine that provides power to the train. A locomotive can be steam, diesel or electric.                                                       |
| **Map**                       | **Mapa**                                | An area/region where cities, industries and stations can be placed. The map also defines the playable area of the simulator.                      |
| **Map Cell**                  | **Célula do Mapa**                      | The smallest unit of distance or space on a simulation map, measured in kilometers via the scale.                                                 |
| **Mixed Industry**            | **Indústria Mista**                     | A type of industry that can deal with various types of cargo.                                                                                     |
| **Overbuilding**              | **Sobreposição de Construção**          | An invalid action where the player tries to build over an existing structure.                                                                     |
| **Passenger**                 | **Passageiro**                          | A type of cargo obtained while playing the simulator.                                                                                             |
| **Player**                    | **Jogador**                             | A person that will be able to manage the railway network in the simulator, including building stations, railway lines, and managing trains.       |
| **Point-of-Route**            | **Ponto da Rota**                       | A stop on a train's route, defined by the station and cargo mode to be applied.                                                                   |
| **Port**                      | **Porte**                               | The act of taking a certain object to another place.                                                                                              |
| **Port Behavior**             | **Comportamento de Portes**             | The behaviour of ports in the simulation, which defines what is importing, exporting, or transforming.                                            |
| **Post Office**               | **Estação de Correios**                 | A station building upgrade that improves mail cargo value or quantity.                                                                            |
| **Primary Sector Industry**   | **Indústria do Setor Primário**         | An industry that generates raw materials, which can be used by other industries.                                                                  |
| **Product Owner**             | **Proprietário do Produto**             | A person that demands all the functionalities that need to be available on the final product.                                                     |
| **Raw Material**              | **Matéria-Prima**                       | A type of cargo produced by primary industries, such as coal, wool, or iron ore.                                                                  |
| **Route**                     | **Rota**                                | A predetermined course that a train follows.                                                                                                      |
| **Save/Load Functionality**   | **Guardar/Carregar Funcionalidade**     | Mechanisms allowing maps, scenarios, or simulations to be saved to and loaded from a file.                                                        |
| **Scale**                     | **Escala**                              | The real-world distance represented by a unit on the map grid.                                                                                    |
| **Scenario**                  | **Cenário**                             | A set of conditions (available on a certain map) that determines the trains that can be used, the time period, available technologies, etc.       |
| **Scenario Restrictions**     | **Restrições do Cenário**               | Rules and limitations applied to a specific map, including time, tech, and historical events.                                                     |
| **Simulator**                 | **Simulador**                           | A system that oversees traffic movement and train routes.                                                                                         |
| **Simulation Pause**          | **Pausa da Simulação**                  | A function that stops the simulation to allow the player to adjust the game state without time progression.                                       |
| **Single Line**               | **Linha Única**                         | A type of railway that allows trains to travel in a single direction.                                                                             |
| **Shortest Route**            | **Rota Mais Curta**                     | The most efficient (minimum distance or cost) path between two stations.                                                                          |
| **Station**                   | **Estação**                             | A designated stop where people and freight are able to enter/leave the train.                                                                     |
| **Station Name Suggestion**   | **Sugestão de Nome da Estação**         | A system-generated name for a new station, based on proximity to the nearest city.                                                                |
| **Station Upgrade**           | **Melhoria da Estação**                 | A building added to a station that improves functionality or cargo management.                                                                    |
| **Statistical Analysis**      | **Análise Estatística**                 | The use of statistical techniques to extract insights from data, such as identifying outliers or trends in profits, passengers, or cargo.         |
| **Steam Locomotive**          | **Locomotiva a Vapor**                  | A type of locomotive that produces its pulling power through a steam engine.                                                                      |
| **Steel Mill**                | **Siderúrgica**                         | A transforming industry that consumes coal and iron ore to produce steel.                                                                         |
| **Supply**                    | **Oferta**                              | The quantity of a cargo available for pickup at a station, based on nearby industries or housing blocks.                                          |
| **Terminal**                  | **Terminal**                            | A major station that has more services than a normal station.                                                                                     |
| **Time Period**               | **Período de tempo**                    | A group of two years, one being the initial year of the simulation, and the other being the final.                                                |
| **Track Maintenance**         | **Manutenção da Linha**                 | A recurring cost calculated based on the length of railway lines.                                                                                 |
| **Train**                     | **Comboio**                             | A powered vehicle used for transporting people or freight on railways.                                                                            |
| **Train Maintenance**         | **Manutenção do Comboio**               | The ongoing cost of operating and servicing a train, derived from the locomotive’s technical specifications.                                      |
| **Transforming Industry**     | **Indústria Transformadora**            | An industry that transforms raw materials into finished products.                                                                                 |
| **Travel**                    | **Viajar**                              | The movement of a train going from station A to station B.                                                                                        |
| **Upgrade Availability Date** | **Data de Disponibilidade de Melhoria** | The simulation date when a building becomes available for construction.                                                                           |
| **XY Coordinates**            | **Coordenadas XY**                      | A numerical pair identifying a specific position on the map.                                                                                      |
